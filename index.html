FAQ

Does it use my microphone?

No. Princess AI captures your computer's system audio (the call) and never your microphone, so it transcribes the other side of the conversation, not you.

Which platforms are supported?

macOS and Windows. System-audio capture and screen answers need screen-recording permission, which the app requests on first use.

How does billing work?

Subscriptions and one-time credit packs via Stripe. Usage is metered in credits — answers, screenshot answers, and minutes of listening. You can manage or cancel anytime from the in-app billing portal.

Where does my data go?

Audio is transcribed by Deepgram and questions are answered by Anthropic's Claude. Your session history is stored locally on your device. Don't submit confidential or sensitive information.

Is it allowed in interviews?

That depends entirely on the rules of the specific interview, exam, or employer — many prohibit AI assistance. Princess AI is built for honest, disclosed use; following those rules is your responsibility.
