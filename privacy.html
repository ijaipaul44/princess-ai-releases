Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 June 2026. This is a starting template — have a lawyer review and adapt it for your business and jurisdiction.

What we collect

Account info from Google sign-in (name, email). Billing records are handled by our payment processor, Stripe; we do not store your full card details. Usage is metered as credits.

Audio, questions, and screenshots

To provide the service, audio you choose to capture is streamed to our speech-to-text provider (Deepgram) for transcription, and your questions and any screenshots you submit are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic / Claude) to generate answers. Please do not capture or submit confidential, regulated, or sensitive information, or audio of people whose consent is required.

Session history

Your sessions — the questions and answers — are stored locally on your device, not on our servers. Deleting a session or the app removes that data from your device.

What we don't do

We don't sell your personal data. We use third parties only as needed to run the service (authentication, transcription, AI answers, payments).

Your choices

You can cancel your subscription at any time from the in-app billing portal, and stop using the app whenever you like. For data requests, contact us below.
